EQUIPMENT & DIAGNOSTICS

Our Cutting-Edge Clinical Technology

Explore the advanced systems we use daily to deliver painless, precise, and highly reliable treatment outcomes.

iTero Digital 3D Scanner

Replaces messy traditional impression trays with fast, accurate 3D intraoral digital scans for Invisalign clear aligners, crowns, and smile designing.

Benefits: No Gagging · High Precision · Instant 3D Preview

RVG Digital Radiography

High-resolution digital X-rays providing instant diagnostic views with up to 90% reduced radiation exposure compared to traditional film X-rays.

Benefits: Ultra-Low Radiation · Immediate Results

Diode & Piezolaser Surgery

Painless laser technology for soft-tissue procedures, gum contouring, root canal disinfection, and ultra-precise ultrasonic bone surgery.

Benefits: Minimal Bleeding · Faster Healing

Class-B Autoclave Sterilization

Strict multi-tier sterilization protocols using vacuum Class-B autoclaves, pouch sealing, and regular biological indicator testing for complete safety.

Benefits: 100% Sterile · Zero Cross-Contamination

Pico & MNRF Aesthetic Lasers

Advanced medical-grade laser systems for picosecond pigment removal, acne scar restructuring (Micro-Needling RF), and skin tightening.

Benefits: Non-Invasive · Visible Glow

Operating Microscope

High-magnification optical systems for endodontics (root canal treatment) and delicate microsurgical aesthetic procedures.

Benefits: Extreme Accuracy · Tooth Preservation
